# Historical Relevance and Context

## Active import window
Approximately the most recent 24 months from `bills.xlsx`.

## Source selection
Import only tabs representing a month/year.
Ignore non-month/year reference/template sheets.
Ignore source columns W onward.

## Household contexts

### Prior context
Georgia home
Through June 2026

### Current context
Florida home
Starts July 2026

## Relevance
Current-context history should dominate prediction for:
- mortgage
- electricity
- water/trash
- HOA
- pool
- lawn
- property services
- other home-specific expenses

Prior-context history may remain relevant for portable obligations such as:
- vehicle payments
- subscriptions
- loans
- mobile phone
- debts
- insurance when the same policy remains materially comparable

## Principle
History can be useful for behavior without being useful for amount forecasting.

Do not treat same-category observations as comparable solely because their labels match.
