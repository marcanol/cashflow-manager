# Product Definition

## Problem
The household currently uses a monthly spreadsheet to decide which paycheck funds which bills, intentionally delay some payments when the liquidity tradeoff is favorable, reserve money for future obligations, and manually reconcile expected bills against actual bank activity.

Traditional budget apps focus on categorization after money is spent. This product focuses on the question:

**What can safely be spent now, what should be paid next, what money must be reserved, and what tradeoffs are being made before the next paycheck?**

## Core product
A forward-looking household cash-flow control system with:
- predictable net-income schedules,
- paycheck-aware bill funding,
- physical and virtual reserves,
- bank transaction reconciliation,
- bill statement reconciliation,
- deterministic cash-flow forecasting,
- payment timing optimization,
- Safe-to-Spend,
- exception-driven workflow,
- later analytics + AI.

## Not in scope
- general ledger accounting
- bookkeeping for businesses
- tax accounting
- gross-pay modeling
- payroll deductions
- full QuickBooks/Great Plains functionality
- AI as authoritative calculator

## Primary workflow
1. Expected income and obligations are generated.
2. Statements confirm variable amounts/due dates.
3. Funds are assigned to obligations/paychecks.
4. Reserves remove committed money from Safe-to-Spend.
5. Optimizer recommends payment dates and transfers.
6. Bank transactions confirm what actually happened.
7. Exceptions require user attention.
8. Historical outcomes improve later analytics.
